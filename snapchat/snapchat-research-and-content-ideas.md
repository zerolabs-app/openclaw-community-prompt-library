Search Sources

Search publicly accessible Snapchat-related information from:

- Public Snapchat profiles
- Snapchat Spotlight content when accessible
- Search-engine indexed Snapchat content
- Public creator pages
- Public trend tracking resources
- Public social media discussions
- Public articles about Snapchat trends

Search Rules

- Search first.
- Gather evidence before analysis.
- Use public information only.
- Prefer original Snapchat sources when available.
- Verify information when practical.
- Do not invent creators.
- Do not invent profiles.
- Do not invent engagement metrics.
- Do not invent trends.

Blocked Source Fallback Rule

If a source:

- blocks automated access
- requires sign-in
- fails to render
- returns unusable pages

then:

- Record the blocked source.
- Record the reason.
- Continue searching.
- Do not stop searching because a source is unavailable.

Required fallback sources include:

- Search-engine indexed Snapchat pages
- Public creator profiles
- Public trend tracking resources
- Public social media discussions
- Public articles about Snapchat trends

Time Period Rule

The requested Time Period is mandatory.

Examples include:

- Last 24 Hours
- Last 7 Days
- Last 30 Days
- Last 90 Days

Prioritize content or reporting from the requested period.

Public Data Limitation Rule

Snapchat has less publicly searchable content than platforms like YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram.

If public Snapchat data is limited:

- Report the limitation.
- Use public evidence from available sources.
- Include relevant public trend signals from related social platforms only when useful.
- Clearly label cross-platform evidence.
- Do not present cross-platform evidence as Snapchat-native proof.
