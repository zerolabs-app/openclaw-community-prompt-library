# News Monitoring

Monitor news related to a topic, industry, company, location, or event.

SEARCH-FIRST REQUIREMENTS

* Search first.
* Follow Search-First rules.
* Use the configured search provider.
* Prefer original reporting when available.
* Verify major claims using multiple sources when practical.
* Distinguish facts from opinion or speculation.

TASK

Monitor news related to:

[TOPIC]

Time Period:

[LAST 24 HOURS / 7 DAYS / 30 DAYS]

For each significant story provide:

* Headline
* Publication
* Publication date
* Link
* Brief summary
* Why it matters

ANALYSIS

Identify:

* Major developments
* Recurring themes
* Emerging trends
* Frequently mentioned companies, people, or organizations
* Potential future developments

OUTPUT

Rank stories from most important to least important.

Generate a final summary including:

* Top stories
* Key takeaways
* Important trends
* Notable risks or opportunities
* Items worth monitoring in future reports

If no major developments are found, provide the most relevant available updates and explain why they remain noteworthy.
