# News Hunter

Monitor news related to a topic, industry, company, location, or event.

User Inputs

- Topic: [USER INPUT]
- Industry: [USER INPUT]
- Company or Organization: [USER INPUT]
- Location or Region: [USER INPUT]
- Time Period: [USER INPUT]
- News Type: [USER INPUT]
- Source Type: [USER INPUT]
- Research Goal: [USER INPUT]
- Minimum Number of Stories: [USER INPUT]

Research Goals

Examples include:

- General News Monitoring
- Industry Monitoring
- Company Monitoring
- Competitor Monitoring
- Market Research
- Risk Monitoring
- Opportunity Discovery
- Trend Monitoring
- Event Monitoring

News Types

Examples include:

- Breaking News
- Business News
- Financial News
- Technology News
- Political News
- Local News
- Industry News
- Product Announcements
- Regulatory News
- Acquisition and Merger News

Source Types

Examples include:

- Major News Publications
- Industry Publications
- Local Publications
- Press Releases
- Company Announcements
- Regulatory Sources
- Government Sources

Search Sources

Search publicly accessible news and reporting sources from:

- News publications
- Industry publications
- Company announcements
- Press releases
- Regulatory sources
- Government sources
- Search-engine indexed news pages

Search Rules

- Search first.
- Gather evidence before analysis.
- Prefer original reporting when available.
- Verify major claims using multiple sources when practical.
- Distinguish facts from opinion or speculation.
- Prefer recently published reporting.
- Do not invent stories.
- Do not invent quotes.
- Do not invent events.

Blocked Source Fallback Rule

If a source:

- blocks automated access
- requires sign-in
- fails to render
- returns unusable pages

then:

- Record the blocked source.
- Record the reason.
- Continue searching.
- Do not stop searching because a source is unavailable.

Required fallback sources include:

- Major news publications
- Industry publications
- Press releases
- Regulatory sources
- Search-engine indexed news pages

Time Period Rule

The requested Time Period is mandatory.

Examples include:

- Last 24 Hours
- Last 7 Days
- Last 30 Days
- Last 90 Days

Prioritize stories published during the requested period.

Required Story Information

For each significant story provide:

- Headline
- Publication
- Publication date
- Story link
- Story category
- Brief summary
- Why it matters

Ranking Rules

- Rank stories from most important to least important.
- Prioritize stories directly related to the requested topic.
- Prioritize original reporting.
- Clearly identify opinion or analysis content.
- Explain why each story is important.

Analysis

Identify:

- Major developments
- Recurring themes
- Emerging trends
- Frequently mentioned companies
- Frequently mentioned people
- Frequently mentioned organizations
- Potential future developments

Output

Generate:

- Top stories
- Key takeaways
- Important trends
- Notable risks
- Notable opportunities
- Items worth monitoring in future reports

Final Summary

Identify:

- Most Important Story
- Most Significant Trend
- Largest Opportunity
- Largest Risk
- Most Important Development To Monitor
- Most Frequently Mentioned Organization

No Results Rule

If no major developments are found:

- Report the limitation.
- Provide evidence of the search performed.
- Explain the limiting factors.
- Provide the most relevant available updates.
- Explain why they remain noteworthy.
- Do not invent stories.
- Do not invent events.
- Do not invent quotes.
