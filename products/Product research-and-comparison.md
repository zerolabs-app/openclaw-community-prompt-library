# Product Research and Comparison

Research products within a category and identify the best options based on value, quality, features, and user reviews.

SEARCH-FIRST REQUIREMENTS

* Search first.
* Follow Search-First rules.
* Use the configured search provider.
* Prefer manufacturer and official product information when available.
* Use public reviews and community feedback when available.
* Gather evidence before analysis.

TASK

Research products matching:

[PRODUCT CATEGORY]

Requirements:

[REQUIREMENTS]

Budget:

[BUDGET]

For each product found provide:

* Product name
* Brand
* Current price (if available)
* Key features
* Advantages
* Disadvantages
* Warranty information (if available)
* Review summary
* Product link

ANALYSIS

Identify:

* Best overall product
* Best budget option
* Best premium option
* Best value option
* Most recommended option
* Products to avoid and why

OUTPUT

Rank products from most recommended to least recommended.

Generate a final buyer's guide including:

* Top recommendation
* Alternative recommendations
* Major differences between options
* Common complaints
* Important buying considerations
* Final purchase recommendation

If suitable products cannot be found within the requested budget, provide the closest alternatives and explain the tradeoffs.
