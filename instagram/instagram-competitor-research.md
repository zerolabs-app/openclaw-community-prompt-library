# Instagram Competitor Hunter

Analyze successful Instagram accounts, content patterns, audience interests, and growth opportunities in the requested niche.

User Inputs

- Topic or Niche: [USER INPUT]
- Target Country or Region: [USER INPUT]
- Time Period: [USER INPUT]
- Content Type: [USER INPUT]
- Research Goal: [USER INPUT]

Research Goals

Examples include:

- Competitor Research
- Creator Discovery
- Content Strategy Research
- Viral Post Research
- Reel Research
- Carousel Research
- Audience Research
- Hashtag Research
- Posting Strategy Research
- Emerging Trend Monitoring

Content Types

Examples include:

- Reels
- Carousel posts
- Photo posts
- Stories
- Educational posts
- Product posts
- Lifestyle content
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Influencer content

Search Sources

Search publicly accessible Instagram content from:

- Public account profiles
- Public post pages
- Public Reel pages
- Public hashtag pages
- Search-engine indexed Instagram content
- Public creator analytics when available
- Public trend tracking resources when available

Search Rules

- Search first.
- Gather evidence before analysis.
- Use public information only.
- Prefer original Instagram account and post sources.
- Prefer recently published content.
- Verify information when practical.
- Do not invent accounts.
- Do not invent posts.
- Do not invent follower counts.
- Do not invent engagement metrics.
- Do not invent trends.

Blocked Source Fallback Rule

If a source:

- blocks automated access
- requires sign-in
- fails to render
- returns unusable pages

then:

- Record the blocked source.
- Record the reason.
- Continue searching.
- Do not stop searching because a source is unavailable.

Required fallback sources include:

- Search-engine indexed Instagram pages
- Public account profiles
- Public hashtag pages
- Public creator analytics resources
- Public trend tracking resources

Time Period Rule

The requested Time Period is mandatory.

Examples include:

- Last 24 Hours
- Last 7 Days
- Last 30 Days
- Last 90 Days
- Last 12 Months

Prioritize content published during the requested period.

Account Size Rule

If account size is specified:

- Prioritize accounts within the requested range.
- Clearly identify out-of-range accounts.
- Explain why they were included.

Required Account Information

For each account provide:

- Account name
- Profile link
- Topic or niche
- Follower count when available
- Estimated audience size
- Primary content type
- Posting frequency when available
- Common content themes
- Engagement indicators
- Notes explaining why the account appears successful

Required Post Information

For important posts provide:

- Account name
- Post or Reel link
- Content type
- Topic
- Caption style
- Visual style
- Engagement indicators
- Notes explaining why the post appears successful

Competitor Analysis

Identify:

- Common content patterns
- Common posting schedules
- Popular content formats
- Common caption styles
- Common visual styles
- Recurring themes
- Audience interests
- Growth opportunities
- Content gaps competitors are missing

Content Opportunities

Identify:

- Underserved topics
- Low-competition opportunities
- High-engagement content formats
- Potential content series
- Hashtag or topic cluster opportunities
- Reels opportunities
- Carousel opportunities

Ranking Rules

- Rank accounts from most useful to least useful as research examples.
- Prioritize accounts with clear public evidence.
- If exact matches are limited, include closest relevant accounts.
- Explain why each account is useful for research.

Output

Generate:

- Top competitor accounts
- Top posts or Reels when available
- Successful content patterns
- Audience insights
- Content gaps
- Content opportunities
- Recommended content ideas
- Suggested posting strategy
- Suggested hashtags or topic clusters if discovered
- Future trends to monitor

Final Summary

Identify:

- Best Competitor To Study

- Most Useful Posting Strategy
- Most Important Trend To Monitor

No Results Rule

If insufficient qualifying accounts or posts are found:

- Report the limitation.
- Provide evidence of the search performed.
- Explain the limiting factors.
- Do not invent accounts.
- Do not invent posts.
- Do not invent follower counts.
