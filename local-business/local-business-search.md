# Local Business Search

Find the best local businesses matching the requested category and location.

SEARCH-FIRST REQUIREMENTS

* Search first.
* Follow Search-First rules.
* Use the configured search provider.
* Prefer official business websites when available.
* Verify information from multiple public sources when possible.

TASK

Search for businesses matching:

[BUSINESS TYPE]

within:

[LOCATION]

For each business found provide:

* Business name
* Website
* Address
* Phone number (if available)
* Business category
* Hours of operation (if available)
* Review summary
* Notable features
* Why it may be a good choice

ANALYSIS

Identify:

* Highest-rated businesses
* Best value options
* Unique offerings
* New or trending businesses
* Businesses that appear most recommended

OUTPUT

Rank results from most promising to least promising.

Generate a final summary including:

* Top recommendations
* Best overall choice
* Best budget-friendly choice
* Best premium choice
* Any notable trends discovered during research
