# Local Business Hunter

Find the best local businesses matching the requested criteria.

User Inputs

- Business Type: [USER INPUT]
- Location: [USER INPUT]
- Maximum Distance: [USER INPUT]
- Minimum Rating: [USER INPUT]

Search Sources

Search publicly accessible business information from:

- Official business websites
- Business directories
- Review platforms
- Maps and local listing services
- Chamber of commerce directories
- Search-engine indexed business listings
- Any publicly accessible local business sources

Search Rules

- Search first.
- Gather evidence before analysis.
- Prefer official business websites.
- Verify information when practical.
- Prefer currently operating businesses.
- Do not invent businesses.
- Do not invent reviews.
- Do not invent ratings.
- Do not invent hours.

Blocked Source Fallback Rule

If a source:

- blocks automated access
- requires sign-in
- fails to render
- returns unusable pages

then:

- Record the blocked source.
- Record the reason.
- Continue searching.
- Do not stop searching because a source is unavailable.

Required fallback sources include:

- Official business websites
- Business directories
- Review platforms
- Search-engine indexed business listings
- Local chamber of commerce listings

Geographic Scope Rule

The requested location is mandatory.

For local business searches:

- Search only inside the requested location.
- Do not substitute different cities.
- Do not substitute different counties.
- Do not substitute different regions.
- Do not expand beyond the requested Maximum Distance.

Businesses outside the permitted search area must not be:

- Ranked.
- Recommended.
- Selected as top choices.

Business Type Rule

The requested business type is mandatory.

Do not substitute different business categories unless explicitly requested.

If no qualifying businesses are found:

- Report that no qualifying businesses were found.
- Do not substitute a different business category.

Required Business Information

For each qualifying business provide:

- Business name
- Website
- Address
- Phone number when available
- Business category
- Hours of operation when available
- Rating when available
- Review count when available
- Price range when available
- Notable features
- Notes explaining why the business may be a good choice

Ranking Rules

- Rank businesses from most promising to least promising.
- Prioritize businesses matching the requested requirements.
- Prioritize businesses with strong public reputation.
- If no strong matches exist, rank the closest alternatives.
- Explain why each alternative may still be worth considering.

Analysis

Identify:

- Highest Rated Business
- Best Value Option
- Best Premium Option
- Most Recommended Business
- Most Unique Offering
- New or Trending Business
- Businesses Worth Contacting First

Market Summary

Summarize:

- local competition
- pricing observations
- common business features
- notable findings discovered during the search

No Results Rule

If no qualifying businesses are found:

- Report that no qualifying businesses were found.
- Provide evidence of the search performed.
- Explain the limiting factors.
- Do not invent businesses.
- Do not invent ratings.
- Do not invent reviews.
- Do not substitute different business categories.
- Do not substitute different locations.
