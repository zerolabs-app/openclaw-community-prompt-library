# YouTube Trend Hunter

Research YouTube channels, videos, audience interests, content strategies, and growth opportunities.

User Inputs

- Topic or Niche: [USER INPUT]
- Target Country or Region: [USER INPUT]
- Time Period: [USER INPUT]
- Content Type: [USER INPUT]
- Research Goal: [USER INPUT]

Research Goals

Examples include:

- Trend Discovery
- Competitor Research
- Viral Video Research
- Content Idea Generation
- Channel Discovery
- Audience Research
- Emerging Trend Monitoring
- Publishing Strategy Research

Content Types

Examples include:

- YouTube Shorts
- Long Form Videos
- Live Streams
- Podcasts
- Educational Content
- Documentary Content
- Tutorial Content
- Commentary Content

Search Sources

Search publicly accessible YouTube content from:

- Channel pages
- Video pages
- Shorts feeds
- Playlist pages
- Search-engine indexed YouTube content
- Public creator analytics when available

Search Rules

- Search first.
- Gather evidence before analysis.
- Prefer original channel and video sources.
- Prefer recently published content.
- Verify information when practical.
- Do not invent channels.
- Do not invent videos.
- Do not invent view counts.
- Do not invent subscriber counts.
- Do not invent trends.

Blocked Source Fallback Rule

If a source:

- blocks automated access
- requires sign-in
- fails to render
- returns unusable pages

then:

- Record the blocked source.
- Record the reason.
- Continue searching.
- Do not stop searching because a source is unavailable.

Required fallback sources include:

- Search-engine indexed YouTube pages
- Public channel pages
- Public creator analytics resources
- Public trend tracking resources

Time Period Rule

The requested Time Period is mandatory.

Examples include:

- Last 24 Hours
- Last 7 Days
- Last 30 Days
- Last 90 Days
- Last 12 Months

Prioritize content published during the requested period.

Channel Size Rule

If channel size is specified:

- Prioritize channels within the requested range.
- Clearly identify out-of-range channels.
- Explain why they were included.

Required Channel Information

For each channel provide:

- Channel name
- Channel link
- Topic or niche
- Subscriber count when available
- Total views when available
- Upload frequency when available
- Content category
- Notes explaining why the channel appears successful

Required Video Information

For each video provide:

- Channel name
- Video title
- Video link
- Publish date
- Video length
- View count when available
- Content format
- Title style
- Thumbnail style
- Notes explaining why the video appears successful
