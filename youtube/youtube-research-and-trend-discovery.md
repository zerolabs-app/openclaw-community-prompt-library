# YouTube Research and Trend Discovery

Research YouTube channels, videos, audience interests, content strategies, and emerging trends.

SEARCH-FIRST REQUIREMENTS

* Search first.
* Follow Search-First rules.
* Use the configured search provider.
* Gather evidence before analysis.
* Prefer original channel and video sources.
* Verify information when practical.

TASK

Research the following YouTube niche or topic:

[TOPIC]

Search for:

* Trending videos
* Fast-growing channels
* High-performing content
* Audience interests
* Emerging trends
* Recurring content patterns

For each channel or video identified provide:

* Channel name
* Channel link
* Video link (if applicable)
* Subscriber count (if available)
* View count (if available)
* Content category
* Why it appears successful

ANALYSIS

Identify:

* Common video formats
* Popular titles
* Thumbnail patterns
* Recurring topics
* Audience interests
* Growth opportunities
* Underserved content areas
* Emerging trends

OUTPUT

Generate a report containing:

* Top channels
* Top videos
* Trending topics
* Successful content patterns
* Recommended content ideas
* Suggested publishing strategy
* Potential future trends to monitor

Common Examples

* YouTube Shorts research
* Educational channels
* AI content
* History content
* Gaming channels
* Business channels
* Travel channels
* Product review channels
* Competitor research
* Trend monitoring

For trend-monitoring tasks, prioritize recent uploads within the requested time period and identify patterns that may indicate future growth opportunities.
