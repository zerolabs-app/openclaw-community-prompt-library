# Jobs & Careers Prompts

This category contains OpenClaw prompts for job searching and application preparation.

## Common Uses

* Searching job boards and company career pages for openings matching specific criteria
* Comparing a job description against your resume
* Tailoring your resume's wording and emphasis to match a specific posting
* Recurring searches for new postings matching your criteria

## Search-First Recommendation

When researching job openings:

1. Search first.
2. Follow Search-First rules.
3. Prefer official job boards and company career pages.
4. Only include real, currently open postings — no expired or closed listings.
5. Use AI analysis after research is complete.

## Example Prompt Types

* Find job openings matching title, location, and salary criteria.
* Compare a job description against a resume and draft a tailored version for that posting.
* Run as a recurring task to catch new postings matching your criteria.

## A Note On Resumes

Tailoring reorganizes and rewords a resume to match a job posting's language — it does not fabricate experience. Always do a final read-through before submitting.
